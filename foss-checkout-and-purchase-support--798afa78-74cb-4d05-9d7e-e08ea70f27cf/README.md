# FOSS · Checkout and purchase support

[View in Linear](https://linear.app/ship-systems/project/foss-checkout-and-purchase-support-30205bed96f6)

Matt Healy · Weeks 6–8 · Verified buy-to-Reps checkout, terms, welcome email, support controls and handoff.

## Why

Deliver the proposal’s buy-to-product journey: purchase, account, terms acceptance, welcome email and access to the Reps. Owner: Matt Healy.

## Calendar

Week 6 (Nov 30–Dec 4): checkout proven in test mode, including successful, failed, refunded and duplicate payments. Week 7 (Dec 7–11): accepted terms and approved welcome email ready before live checkout; live purchase/refund once Matt Foss approves price, terms and refund policy. Week 8 (Dec 14–18): optional report-an-issue button and Save button check; final retests and written handoff before the final demonstration.

## Proposal rows

Core: Checkout proven in test mode; Checkout live; Click-to-accept terms; Welcome email; Retests and handoff notes.

Optional: Report-an-issue button; Save button check. Optional rows apply only if retained in the signed proposal.

## Done when

A verified test purchase runs from buy button to first rep; failed and duplicate cases are safe. Live checkout has explicit client approvals and evidence of one real purchase/refund. Terms version/time are stored per buyer; the approved welcome email arrives correctly. Retained support controls work and the final verification/handoff is delivered.

## Dependencies

FOSS Group-provided terms, approved wording/prices/refund policy, test and live Stripe readiness, existing account/entitlement system and redesigned pages.

## Next phase not here

Coaching upsells, contracts above a price threshold, lifetime access, refund admin screens and buyer dashboard. Source: FOSS Product Launch proposal, 28 October 2026, Section F.
